About C2PA Cleaner V2

A local-first image provenance inspector and lossless metadata export tool. Engineered to inspect provenance signals and remove C2PA Content Credentials manifests without re-encoding visual pixels.

Strict Local Privacy Guarantee

"Your images are processed locally on this device. They are not uploaded to a cloud service unless you explicitly choose to open an external verification tool."

Two Fundamentally Distinct Provenance Technologies

1. C2PA Content Credentials (Metadata)

Standardized by C2PA. Stored in container header segments (JPEG APP11 JUMBF, PNG caBX chunk, WebP C2PA chunk). Contains cryptographic claims, assertions, and author signatures. Can be inspected and removed losslessly on your device without altering visual pixels.

2. SynthID (Content-Level Watermark)

Developed by Google DeepMind and adopted by OpenAI / DALL-E. An imperceptible watermark embedded directly into visual pixel values across frequency bands. Survives compression, cropping, and format conversions. Cannot be detected or removed locally without an authorized detector model.

Truthful Boundaries & Guarantees

C2PA Cleaner adheres strictly to engineering integrity and clear product limits:

✕
Removing C2PA does NOT remove SynthID: Stripping file container metadata leaves the visual pixel bitstream 100% byte-identical. Any imperceptible content-level watermark remains untouched.
✕
No fabricated watermark claims: If an external authorized detector has not evaluated an image, SynthID status is accurately reported as "Not checked". We never claim an image is watermark-free.
✕
No invisible pixel watermark removal: We do not perform destructive image transformations or lossy re-encoding to attempt watermark bypass.
✕
No remote cloud database erasure: Cloud provenance stores or external records held on third-party servers cannot be altered by a local desktop tool.
